about

  
From models to agents to products.

  

    

      
I'm an AI/ML engineer with a strong foundation in Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Python backend development. I build and evaluate models with PyTorch and TensorFlow, and I work hands-on with Generative AI and LLMs.

      
Lately my focus is agentic AI — systems that take a goal, make a plan, call tools, check their own results and recover when something breaks. I care about the boring parts that make AI trustworthy: validation, tests, tracing and honest evaluation.

      
I've applied this in real products like Progly and Koshbook — AI implementation, prompt engineering, data maintenance and deployment.
